# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Ren serves people learning or solving something inside another application: students watching lessons, makers working in CAD or slicers, developers reading code, and anyone who needs a knowledgeable guide for what is currently on screen. Their primary task remains in the host application. Ren should feel present and immediately reachable without pulling them into a separate workspace.

## Product Purpose

Ren is an ambient, screen-aware teacher. A learner points, speaks naturally, and receives a concise explanation plus temporary visual guidance directly over their work. Success means there are no subject modes, tool installation steps, source pickers, or repeated permission prompts between confusion and understanding.

## Brand Personality

Calm, perceptive, and encouraging. Ren behaves like an excellent teacher sitting nearby: attentive when invited, quiet otherwise, confident without pretending certainty, and warm without becoming childish.

## Anti-references

Ren must not resemble a conventional chatbot window, a dense AI dashboard, a floating customer-support widget, or a decorative glass panel full of controls. It should not expose model plumbing, subject tools, or mode selection. Clicky and OpenClicky are interaction references for their compact companion overlay and visible state changes, not visual identities to copy.

## Design Principles

1. Stay inside the learner's flow instead of becoming another destination.
2. Make listening, looking, thinking, and responding instantly legible.
3. Keep the collapsed presence tiny and the expanded experience focused.
4. Point and demonstrate whenever a visual explanation is clearer than prose.
5. Earn trust through visible privacy state, restraint, and honest capability.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for text, controls, focus indication, and contrast. Every interaction must remain available by keyboard and text when voice is unavailable. Respect reduced-motion preferences, never encode state through color alone, provide screen-reader names and live regions, and preserve usable contrast over unpredictable desktop backgrounds.
